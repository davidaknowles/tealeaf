# Isoform-switch search and PSI evidence

This analysis searches the selected fixed-total \(A=64\) full-data omnibus path-usage fits for blocks whose dominant fitted path changes across cell types. It ranks each significant block by the largest range of a path's across-subject mean usage. Among 791 significant blocks, 92 change dominant path, but no path has a cell-type mean at most 0.1 in one cell type and at least 0.9 in another. The figures therefore show the three largest unique-gene, non-whole-gene dominant-path changes rather than claiming complete 0-to-1 switches.

The selected blocks are Agtpbp1 B1, Dlg4 B1, and Ntrk2 B1. Their largest production path-usage ranges are 0.326--0.639, 0.179--0.430, and 0.255--0.480. The switch_ranking.tsv table retains the complete significant-block ranking, selected_switches.tsv records the plotted subset, and the path-usage tables retain the subject-level estimates and plotted summaries.

Each event has separate poly(dT) and random-hexamer plotnine heatmaps. P columns show the production model's fitted across-subject mean path usage. E columns show empirical exon PSI, defined for a variable exon as its pooled mean aligned depth divided by the mean depth of the nearest path-shared exon on each available side and clipped to \([0,1]\). J columns show empirical junction PSI, defined as the junction's pooled UMI count divided by the total pooled count of all unique observed junctions sharing either its donor or acceptor site. Competitor junctions are retained when their other endpoint lies outside the displayed block. Zero denominators are missing. Exons and junctions present in every displayed path are omitted; constitutive exons are still used as exon-depth references.

Path usage and empirical PSI share one fixed 0--1 color scale. Exons and junctions occur in 5'-to-3' RNA order within each path, shared nonconstitutive features may appear under multiple paths, and E/J identifiers are gene-global within a displayed block. The TSV beside each pair of PDFs retains exact values, genomic coordinates, feature types, path membership, and display order. The empirical PSI panels are read-support summaries, not inputs to or reconstructions of the Tealeaf test.

The dataset-specific workflow is extra_scripts/plot_microglia_isoform_switches.py. Reusable BAM support, PSI, path-usage, and heatmap functions are in tealeaf/sc/sashimi.py. The first pass streams the BAMs with an environment containing pysam and can use --skip-plots; --plot-only then renders saved matrices with plotnine without reopening BAMs.

The absence of a near-complete switch under the selected fixed-total concentration \(A=64\) is not evidence that no biological switch exists. The production prior is optimized for testing-level evidence and regularizes each subject--cell-type estimate toward uniform usage. In a post-selection sensitivity over the 791 production-significant blocks, \(A=2\) still yields no cell-type mean path spanning 0.1--0.9, whereas the unregularized fit yields three. The unregularized result is unstable, however, with 99 failed blocks, 1st and 5th percentiles exactly zero, 95th and 99th percentiles exactly one, and two of the three apparent mean switches having their low-usage extreme estimated from only one subject. `smoothing_sensitivity.tsv` records the diagnostic. The plotted \(A=64\) values should therefore be read as regularized production estimates, not unbiased endpoints or a test for complete switching.
