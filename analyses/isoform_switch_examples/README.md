# Dominant-path changes and PSI evidence

Statistical selection uses the production omnibus test with empirical-Bayes-selected fixed-total concentration \(A_{\mathrm{test}}=64\). Effect sizes and displayed path usage are independently refitted with \(A_{\mathrm{report}}=1\). This changes neither calibrated p-values nor the set of 791 BH-significant blocks. It reduces the strong compression of the production test fit while retaining all 1,781 estimable blocks and avoiding the 99 failures and boundary pileup of the unregularized estimator.

Among production-significant blocks, the \(A_{\mathrm{report}}=1\) individual path-usage 1st, 5th, 95th, and 99th percentiles are 0.003, 0.010, 0.959, and 0.986. Estimates are stable to nearby weak concentrations. Across all matched subject--cell-type--path estimates, \(A=1\) versus \(A=0.5\) has Pearson correlation 0.997 and mean absolute difference 0.021; \(A=1\) versus \(A=2\) has correlation 0.995 and mean absolute difference 0.027. The choice of one is a reporting convention selected after this sensitivity check, not an additional inferential tuning step.

The figures show the three unique-gene, non-whole-gene production discoveries with the largest \(A_{\mathrm{report}}=1\) cell-type mean path-usage spans among blocks whose dominant fitted path changes. They are Agtpbp1 B1, Dlg4 B1, and Dclk1 B2. Their largest path-usage ranges are 0.344--0.965, 0.258--0.836, and 0.171--0.739. `switch_ranking.tsv` retains the complete ranking, `selected_switches.tsv` records the plotted subset, and the path-usage tables retain subject-level estimates and summaries.

Each event has separate poly(dT) and random-hexamer plotnine heatmaps. P columns show mean weakly regularized path usage. E columns show empirical variable-exon PSI, defined as pooled mean aligned depth divided by the mean depth of the nearest path-shared exon on each available side and clipped to \([0,1]\). J columns show empirical junction PSI, defined as the junction's pooled UMI count divided by the total pooled count of all unique observed junctions sharing either its donor or acceptor site. Competitor junctions are retained when their other endpoint lies outside the displayed block. Zero denominators are missing. Exons and junctions present in every displayed path are omitted; constitutive exons remain exon-depth references.

Path usage and empirical PSI share one fixed 0--1 color scale. Exons and junctions occur in 5'-to-3' RNA order within each path, shared nonconstitutive features may appear under multiple paths, and E/J identifiers are gene-global within a displayed block. The TSV beside each pair of PDFs retains exact values, genomic coordinates, feature types, path membership, and display order. The empirical PSI values summarize read support; they are not inputs to or reconstructions of the Tealeaf test.

`smoothing_sensitivity.tsv` records the boundary, convergence, and switch diagnostics for \(A\in\{0,0.5,1,2,64\}\). No smoothed setting produces a cell-type mean path spanning 0.1--0.9. The unsmoothed estimator produces three, but two have their low-usage extreme estimated from only one subject. The analysis therefore reports large dominant-path changes rather than claiming complete switches.

The dataset-specific workflow is `extra_scripts/plot_microglia_isoform_switches.py`. Reusable BAM support, PSI, path-usage, and heatmap functions are in `tealeaf/sc/sashimi.py`.
