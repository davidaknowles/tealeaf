# Local-path and alignment-evidence concordance

This analysis asks whether the cell-type ordering implied by statistically significant Tealeaf path-usage estimates is visible in a separate, alignment-level summary of the underlying reads. It includes all full-data omnibus discoveries with at least one explicit local block endpoint anchor. Whole-gene complex blocks without a local anchor are not eligible for this diagnostic.

The alignment scan retains uniquely mapped primary alignments and deduplicates by cell barcode, UMI, genomic start, and CIGAR. Results remain separate for poly(dT) and random-hexamer half-cells. The displayed path estimates come from the fixed-total \(A_{\rm report}=1\) reporting refit; discovery status remains determined by the calibrated \(A_{\rm test}=64\) omnibus analysis.

Variable exonic regions are split at every tested-path boundary, which prevents the sequence shared by alternative donor or acceptor isoforms from being counted as discriminating evidence. For each subject, cell type, and primer, variable-segment depth and variable-junction counts are each divided by mean aligned depth over the block's explicit endpoint anchor exon or exons. The within-feature rank comparison is invariant to the remaining feature-specific unit difference. The complementary contrast table also records exon ratios clipped to \([0,1]\) and junction PSI against all unique observed junctions sharing the donor or acceptor site, including competitors whose other endpoint lies outside the block.

The primary plot ranks cell types separately by fitted inclusion and alignment-derived inclusion for every support-qualified variable feature. A feature requires at least three cell types, total endpoint depth of at least 20 per cell type, and a fitted cell-type range of at least 0.05. Feature-level Spearman correlations are averaged within each block so every significant block contributes one point per feature class and primer. The horizontal zero line is the no-directional-agreement reference. Bootstrap intervals resample blocks.

The complete uniquely mapped, UMI-deduplicated scan contains 933,478 subject--cell-type--primer--feature observations from 750 anchored significant blocks and 5,064 variable features. After support filtering, the rank analysis retains 115 poly(dT) and 288 random-hexamer blocks for exonic segments, with median within-block correlations 0.181 (95% block-bootstrap interval 0.029--0.302) and 0.117 (0.057--0.246). The corresponding junction analysis retains 76 and 190 blocks, with medians 0.269 (0.181--0.389) and 0.260 (0.146--0.333). Positive correlations occur in 60.9%, 57.3%, 71.1%, and 66.3% of blocks, respectively; exact one-sided sign-test p-values are 0.0124, 0.00777, 0.000156, and \(4.04\times10^{-6}\). Thus agreement is modest for exon depth and stronger for junctions, but every primer-by-feature stratum shifts in the expected direction.

The contrast table provides a complementary magnitude diagnostic. For every variable feature and primer, it selects the largest fitted difference among cell-type pairs with at least three common support-qualified subjects, without inspecting raw evidence, and then calculates the raw high-minus-low contrast in those subjects.

These are same-read descriptive checks, not independent replication and not inputs to test calibration. Agreement establishes that the fitted changes have a recognizable alignment-level counterpart; it does not supply an independent p-value.

The dataset-specific workflow is `extra_scripts/plot_microglia_path_coverage_concordance.py`. Reusable path-feature segmentation, alignment collection, UMI deduplication, and subject-level evidence summaries are in `tealeaf/sc/sashimi.py`.
