# Primer-separated sashimi plots

These plots provide read-level support for three characterized cell-type differential-splicing events selected by the production omnibus analysis. App block B3 is the exon-15 cassette, Gria2 block B1 is the flip/flop mutually exclusive pair, and Grin1 block B3 is the C1 cassette.

Inferential selection retains \(A_{\mathrm{test}}=64\). Within each block, the displayed pair must pass the production pairwise BH threshold and is chosen by the largest \(A_{\mathrm{report}}=1\) mean paired ILR-difference norm. This selects cortical excitatory versus hippocampal pyramidal cells for App, norm 1.152 and testing-fit FDR 0.0211; cortical excitatory versus midbrain inhibitory cells for Gria2, norm 0.294 and FDR 0.00105; and medium-spiny versus midbrain inhibitory cells for Grin1, norm 1.498 and FDR 0.00577. The reporting refit changes neither significance nor FDR.

The workflow streams 16 spliced-pseudobulk BAM files. Alignments are assigned to poly(dT) or random-hexamer priming by the paired half-cell barcode map. Coverage and junction arcs are pooled within cell type and primer, divided by the number of primer-specific half-cells, and multiplied by 1,000. Introns are compressed, and the bottom annotation contains only the paths tested by Tealeaf.

The sashimi tracks are descriptive checks rather than inputs to the statistical test. They omit unspliced and exonic-only EC observations and do not resolve ambiguous reads through the primer-specific EC maps. Grouped bars report \(A_{\mathrm{report}}=1\) omnibus-refit mean path proportions and standard errors across paired subjects. The omnibus reporting refit gives each subject--cell-type combination one estimate independent of the displayed pair. These estimates jointly use both primer-specific likelihoods and are separated by cell type and path rather than primer.

The selected-contrast, spliced-UMI support, cell-count, and path-usage tables retain the audit data. The manifest records both testing and reporting concentrations, effect norms, testing-fit FDR values, coordinates, and figure paths. Reusable BAM summarization is in `tealeaf/sc/sashimi.py`; dataset-specific selection and plotting are in `extra_scripts/plot_microglia_priming_sashimi.py`.
