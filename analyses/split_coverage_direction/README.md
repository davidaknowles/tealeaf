# Coverage and split-direction audit

This audit uses the selected concentration-32 merged Tealeaf results from Table 1, without changing discovery p-values or reselecting smoothing. Coverage is the saved median modeled gene UMI count per pseudobulk within each tested cell-type contrast. Both methods use this same EC-derived gene-coverage proxy in their matched universe; it is not a method-specific count of informative junctions or event reads. A gene's coverage is the median across its shared cell-type pairs, with each pair counted once rather than once per block or event.

`coverage_correlations.tsv` reports Spearman correlation of p-values, not negative log p-values, with coverage in each fold. Negative rho means smaller p-values at greater coverage. Event-level values use each method's feature tests. Gene-level values use Table 1's within-pair and across-pair Simes aggregation. Partial rank correlations residualize event ranks on the reference paired-subject count and gene ranks on that count and the number of feature tests. These are descriptive controls, not a causal analysis or a replacement test.

`null_coverage_correlations.tsv` repeats the correlation audit in each of 32 existing fixed-concentration sign-flip families. `null_coverage_bins.tsv` reports calibrated null rejection within coverage quintiles for all tests and separately for two-path and multi-path blocks. These nulls hold the selected concentration fixed rather than repeating EB selection; the calibration pool and null diagnostic pool also overlap. Global calibration does not imply calibration conditional on coverage. Event dependence and the finite number of null families preclude treating every null p-value as an independent Bernoulli observation.

For direction assessment, both folds must test the same feature and cell-type pair. Each fold's event BH correction uses all of its eligible events on that comparator's matched gene--pair universe before intersecting feature identities across folds. The primary selection is the union of events passing event BH at 0.05 in either fold. Intersection, one-fold-selected, and gene-BH-union sensitivities are also saved. The gene-BH-union sensitivity includes all common events in genes significant in either fold under Table 1's aggregation, including weak events; it is not the same selection as event BH.

Tealeaf directions are recovered by rerunning the existing local-path estimator with the same concentration, prior center, and total-concentration scaling, without generating new nulls. The pooled-baseline optimizer is recovered from the archived revision used for Table 1. The October analytic-gradient implementation changes some fitted baselines and failed the original-effect check, so its directions are not attached to September p-values. Recovered mean effect norms must agree with the published values within 1e-6. Published calibrated p-values remain authoritative. Mean ILR effects are mapped to centered log-path effects and aligned by exact path signature; incompatible feature sets are excluded and counted. Two-path direction agreement is ordinary sign agreement. For multi-path blocks, agreement means positive inner product of the aligned effect vectors. Scalar SUPPA2 effects use paired mean PSI direction, while the hybrid uses its saved testing ILR direction. All contrasts are oriented from canonical level a to b. Zero effects have no evaluable direction.

LeafCutter and scQuint directions are recovered from the common sparse junction-count bundle using the existing long-read audit's cluster/group mappings and batched mean-composition implementation. Only the samples listed in each split's contrast manifest enter that split's equal-pseudobulk mean proportions. Zero-total pseudobulks do not enter the corresponding group mean. The b-minus-a effect vector is aligned by junction identity across folds. MAJIQ uses its native b-minus-a median PSI for each tested edge, matched by the exact feature identifiers used when normalizing its p-value output; distinct edge tests are not collapsed to an LSV. Signed effects are attached without dropping missing effects or changing the published tests before BH correction.

`direction_summary.tsv` reports event-weighted and equal-gene-weighted agreement, cosine similarity, and coverage-bin breakdowns. `direction_status.tsv` identifies methods lacking recoverable signed effects; missing directions must not be inferred from their nonnegative test statistics. `event_direction_agreement.tsv.gz` retains event-level audit records. The Table 1 comparator union agreements are LeafCutter 142/155 (91.6%), MAJIQ 1,750/1,831 (95.6%) among 1,845 selected common edges, scQuint 549/585 (93.8%), SUPPA2 378/405 (93.3%), primer-aware SUPPA2 105/114 (92.1%), and the hybrid 590/611 (96.6%). Agreement among selected events is descriptive and does not prove FDR control or eliminate systematic artifacts shared across subject halves.

`table1_gene_metrics.tsv` recomputes the shared-universe gene-level replication metrics from the same eligible saved tests used for the directional and coverage audits. The current converged, finite-p-value MAJIQ and scQuint universes contain 2,499 pairs / 479 genes and 2,599 pairs / 506 genes, respectively, one pair and gene fewer than their older printed denominators. Their replicated gene counts are unchanged. Table 1 now uses these internally consistent complete-case counts and rates. The historical rMATS row is retained separately, its aggregate split metrics survive but its split event tables and exact shared event universe have not been recovered. Both direction cells in that row are marked unavailable; this does not mean zero agreement, and the paired full-data rMATS results are not a substitute for split effects.

Numerically unstable archived refits are excluded from directional evaluation, not from the published p-value or coverage analyses. The magnitude tolerance is not relaxed. `direction_refit_checks.tsv` and fold-specific norm-diagnostic tables record every check, including excluded counts. Direction summaries retain the full number of selected common events and the smaller evaluable denominator. Agreement estimates therefore describe the archive-compatible subset rather than assuming directions for unreproduced events.

On the SUPPA2-matched universe, Tealeaf gene-level p--coverage rho is -.427/-.323 versus SUPPA2 -.178/-.262. Tealeaf union-event directional agreement is 1,092/1,107 (98.6%) among 1,171 selected common events, with equal-gene-weighted agreement 97.2%. Binary signs give 774/784 (98.7%) and multi-path directions 318/323 (98.5%). Using the original published fold-event BH cutoffs instead gives 1,003/1,014 (98.9%). All events in the gene-BH union give 1,852/2,172 (85.3%). Native SUPPA2 gives 378/405 (93.3%) under matched event-BH union selection; primer-aware SUPPA2 gives 105/114 (92.1%) and the hybrid gives 590/611 (96.6%). Signed junction-comparator summaries and historical split rMATS feature inputs are not included.

Coverage-stratified null rejection is not uniform despite pooled calibration. Lowest/highest quintiles reject at .0341/.0615 and .0435/.0576 at nominal .05 in folds 0/1; the highest quintile rejects at .00273/.00235 at nominal .001. This is a coverage-dependent calibration warning. The observed correlation and strong split-direction agreement do not establish that the discovery advantage is entirely genuine power, nor quantify how much is due to conditional calibration. A coverage-aware calibration comparison would require a new analysis, not relabeling these diagnostics as a fix.
