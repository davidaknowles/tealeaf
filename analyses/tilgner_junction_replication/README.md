# Junction-method replication in independent long reads

This directory compares full-data cell-type discoveries from Tealeaf, LeafCutter, relaxed-coverage MAJIQ Heterogen, scQuint, and a paired junction-CLR ablation with the P56 mouse-brain ScISOr-Seq2 transcript-UMI matrix from Joglekar et al. (2024). Every method is evaluated by directional agreement with the same independent long-read source under common mapping and depth rules. These are conditional validation rates, not a matched comparison of power.

The MAJIQ result uses a combined build requiring support in at least three pseudobulks and PSI coverage of at least three reads in at least three bins. Heterogen requires coverage in at least half the pseudobulks within each compared group. Across the 28 contrasts among the eight mapped cell types, MAJIQ tests 10,297 edges and calls 2,657 edges from 466 genes. Significant edges are restored to multijunction LSVs before estimating and validating direction.

The discovery summaries remain significance-selected. The rank plot now uses separate all-tested source mappings for LeafCutter and scQuint in `all_tested`, and for MAJIQ in `all_tested/majiq`, without a significance cutoff. The common source-mapping and depth rules retain 372, 1,830, and 385 associations respectively, so all three have 200 plotted points. Their top-100 agreement is 76%, 77%, and 67%, with normalized rank-curve area 86.5%, 82.5%, and 81.9%. Nonsignificant inputs extend the curves rather than being called discoveries.

`sign_agreement_rank_summary.tsv` records top-40, top-100, and top-200 agreement and the normalized discrete area, the mean cumulative agreement over integer ranks through the cutoff. Short curves have unavailable metrics rather than extrapolation. The Tealeaf omnibus curve now ranks the complete EB-selected production table after source eligibility, using its own raw tail and statistic rather than a selected pairwise statistic. This corrects both a previously pre-truncated input and the omnibus tie-break. Current production omnibus / pairwise top-100 areas are 64.8% / 65.6%; the former omnibus area of 74.1% is superseded. Tealeaf reporting/ranking sensitivities are separate exploratory results under `../tilgner_long_read_replication/ranking_audit`, not changes to production statistical tests.

`method_comparison_summary.tsv` contains the headline comparison, and `method_comparison.pdf` visualizes it. `sign_agreement_by_rank.pdf` shows cumulative long-read sign agreement across the top 200 evaluable associations per method, ranked by calibrated p-value with continuous raw-p-value and statistic tie-breaks. It includes a block-ranked Tealeaf omnibus curve, the overlapping isoform-usage-ratio baseline audit, full-data SUPPA2 events, primer-aware SUPPA2 events, and paired-JCEC rMATS events. `sign_agreement_by_rank.tsv` contains the plotted points. `zeta_sensitivity.tsv` reports the fixed-concentration discovery counts and long-read replication after full source remapping for every tested zeta; `zeta_full_remap.tsv` contains the per-zeta mapped test rows. `junction_replication.tsv.gz` contains the junction-method audit rows, `junction_replication_summary.tsv` summarizes them, `paired_clr_discoveries.tsv.gz` caches the CLR discoveries, `isoform_ratio_pairwise.tsv` contains the pooled ratio-baseline pairwise calls, `majiq_relaxed_contrasts.json` records the rerun contrast manifest, and `manifest.json` records analysis choices. Event-level SUPPA2 and rMATS source tables are under `analyses/comparator_suppa_rmats`.
