# Monte Carlo null-replicate sensitivity

This analysis compares nested null budgets \(R_{\rm null}=32,64,128\) for the primary omnibus cell-type analysis, condition-within-cell-type analysis, full pairwise localization analysis, and both pairwise subject-split folds. Each analysis was run once with 128 deterministic null replicate indices and merged using the first 32, 64, or 128, so differences isolate Monte Carlo budget. `extra_scripts/merge_paired_path_test.py --max-null-replicates` implements the nested truncation.

`summary.tsv` records discovery and null-family summaries. `stability.tsv` compares calibrated p-values and BH sets with the 32-replicate result. Calibrated \(-\log_{10}p\) ranks have Spearman correlation of at least 0.9994, median absolute p-value changes are at most 0.00221, and BH-set Jaccard similarity is at least 0.972. Omnibus BH counts are 583, 586, and 585; condition counts are zero throughout; full pairwise counts are 2,225, 2,226, and 2,228. The separately generated production omnibus run calls 584 blocks.

`reproducibility.tsv` repeats the matched comparator scoring. Tealeaf replicated-gene counts at 32, 64, and 128 are 50, 50, and 50 against LeafCutter; 103, 104, and 103 against MAJIQ Heterogen; and 101, 98, and 99 against scQuint. `replicated_null_audit.tsv` propagates each null family through within-pair and across-pair Simes aggregation, two-fold conjunction, and BH. None of 128 families produces a replicated gene on any comparator-matched universe.

The larger audit finds rare fold-level or full-data null BH calls that were absent among the first 32 replicates. At 128, 4 omnibus families make one BH call, one full pairwise family makes two, and 15 and two families make calls in pairwise folds 0 and 1, with maxima of two and four. No condition family makes a BH call. The observed discovery and replicated-gene conclusions are stable at the default 32, but 32 whole-family realizations are insufficient to characterize rare family-level failures; 128 is more informative for that audit.
